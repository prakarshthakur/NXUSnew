# NXUS Ecosystem: Project Overview 🚀
"eBay for Fun — Fun for Everyone"

## 1. The Vision
NXUS is a hyper-local, experience-driven marketplace designed specifically for the university ecosystem. While platforms like Eventbrite or Facebook Events feel formal and noisy, NXUS brings the "gamified" simplicity of modern apps to the world of student life.

### Our Tagline
> **"ebay for fun."** — Finding your next move should be as easy as swiping a card.

---

## 2. Core Products

### 📱 NXUS App (Radar)
Our flagship mobile experience. NXUS Radar reimagines event discovery using a "swipe-to-match" interface.
- **Discover**: Swipe through "cards" of student-hosted events, parties, pre-games, and study groups.
- **Host**: Anyone can become a host in seconds. List your event, set capacity, and manage your "guest list" directly.
- **Connect**: A built-in logic matches hosts with interested guests, creating a curated social community.

### 🛡️ Redacta
The privacy sibling of NXUS. Redacta is an AI-powered redaction tool built for students and professionals who value their privacy. 
- **AI-Driven**: Instant obfuscation of PII (Personally Identifiable Information) in documents.
- **Web-First**: Blazing fast, browser-based utility that integrates into the NXUS suite.

---

## 3. Technology Stack 🛠️

We’ve built NXUS with a focus on speed, modularity, and cross-platform compatibility:

- **Frontend**: Vanilla JavaScript & CSS (optimized for "Liquid Glass" aesthetics).
- **Backend**: Firebase for real-time authentication and data synchronization.
- **Platform**: Capacitor/Progressive Web App (PWA) architecture — allowing us to deploy to Web, iOS, and Android simultaneously.
- **Analytics**: GA4 integration for deep insights into user engagement.

---

## 4. Key Differentiators 🌟

1. **Gamified Discovery**: Moves away from lists/calendars. Discovery happens through a prioritized "Radar" feed.
2. **Student-First Logic**: Features like "University Email Verification" (Coming Soon) ensure a safe, closed ecosystem for campus life.
3. **Hyper-Local Focus**: Designed to work best within a 5-mile radius of major university hubs.
4. **Integration**: A unified ecosystem where social discovery (NXUS) meets privacy tools (Redacta).
